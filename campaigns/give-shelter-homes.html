Give Shelter Homes

The Give Shelter Homes campaign was organized by Durham Islamic Centre to help provide temporary shelter and housing support for families facing hardship in our community. Many families experiencing housing insecurity turned to the masjid for guidance and assistance.

Donations raised through this campaign went toward emergency housing assistance, rental support, and essential household items to help families get back on their feet.

What This Campaign Supported

  • 🏠 Emergency and temporary housing assistance
  • 🛏️ Essential household and bedding items for new homes
  • 🤝 Rental support for families in transition
  • 📋 Guidance connecting families to community resources

This campaign has ended, but Durham Islamic Centre continues to support families facing housing hardship through community outreach.
