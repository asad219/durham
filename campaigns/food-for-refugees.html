Food For Refugees

Durham Islamic Centre partnered with local community members to provide food relief for refugee families settling in the Durham Region. This campaign helped supply essential groceries and prepared meals to families adjusting to a new country while facing food insecurity.

Donations collected were used to purchase halal groceries, staple foods, and warm meals, distributed directly to families in need with the help of community volunteers.

What This Campaign Provided

  • 🍚 Staple grocery items such as rice, oats, and cereals
  • 🍞 Bread, water, and prepared meals for families
  • 🧥 Winter clothing and blankets for newly arrived families
  • 💊 Basic medicine and hygiene supplies

This campaign has ended, but Durham Islamic Centre continues to support families in need through ongoing community relief efforts.
