About us

Serving the community since 2012

Durham Islamic Centre is a CRA registered charity serving Whitby and surrounding areas with a welcoming place for worship, education and community connection.

2012 Organization founded
2013 First musallah established
2017 Country Lane property acquired
2023 Iqra Masjid opened
