About Us

Serving the Community Since 2012

Durham Islamic Centre is a CRA registered charity organization serving the Muslim community of Whitby and surrounding areas since 2012, providing a welcoming space for worship, education, and community connection.

Our Milestones

2012

Organization Founded

Durham Islamic Centre officially established as a registered charity.

2013

Musallah Established

First prayer space opened to serve the growing local community.

2017

New Property Acquired

Purchased the current property at Country Lane to build our future masjid.

2023

Facility Opening

Iqra Masjid opened its doors for daily Salah in January 2023.
